# 87. The canonical wire encoding, and the vectors that make it testable

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-08-01
- **This is ADR-0084 §5's step 2b, inserted between the surface ADR and the
  triad.** §6 states the sequencing and why this ADR cannot wait for the wire
  lane it was originally assigned to.
- **It ratifies the bytes, and nothing else.** No method, no field, no type, no
  setting. ADR-0085 fixes *what* crosses the wire; this fixes *how it is
  spelled*.
- **No implementation lands with it.** No `src/`, no `tests/`.
