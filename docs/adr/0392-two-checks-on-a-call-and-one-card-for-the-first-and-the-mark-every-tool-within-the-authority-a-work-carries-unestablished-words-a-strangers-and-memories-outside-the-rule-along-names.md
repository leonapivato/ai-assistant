## Context

ADR-0367 §4:1 checks every call against lines for the person whose authority a work carries;
ADR-0391 §3:2 runs it for everyone in the work's say, at the level of its context (ADR-0371 §6:2),
and ADR-0379 §2 gives its outcome. Two outcomes need a yes: a shortfall, whose card goes to the
person whose authority the work carries (ADR-0367 §5:3), and a grant that asks, which needs the yes
of whoever the call's thing belongs to (ADR-0367 §5:6, ADR-0381 §6). ADR-0391 §3:3 sends the first
of those cards also where another in the say is the reason a call is not allowed. ADR-0367 §4:6
keeps ADR-0021 §5's floor. ADR-0368 §8 offers a work only what its authority **could use**. Beside
the check, ADR-0370 §5 checks every send against the work's mark, and §5:8 asks the mark's yes
first. ADR-0391 gives unestablished forwarded words and words a model marks relayed a say of
**none**, which takes the whole work's authority away (§3:2). ADR-0390 §5:5 keeps a record's score
inside the score of each record it names.

The review put to the owner that a message to a hub person allowed only by its default grant
(ADR-0367 §12:1) may take a card each time (the scenarios document's GAP-01). The owner found it
backwards, and ruled two checks on a call, in order, and which tools a work is shown. Reading
ADR-0390, ADR-0391 and this draft, they ruled three open points and corrected two merged texts
(§12).

**Words.** A line, grant, limit, bar, level, the check, a card and a yes are ADR-0367's; a grant
**allows** or **asks** as ADR-0379 has it; a say, a stranger and the relay mark are ADR-0391's; the
mark ADR-0370's; a score and a stored record ADR-0390's; a **memory** ADR-0328 §2's one memory
kind, the memory store's `Memory`, as against its episodes. The **first card** is §2:2's, the
**second card** §2:4's, and a work's **ceiling** §4:1's. A bare section reference, such as §2:1,
is to this decision.

## Decision

We will check a call in two steps, in order, with one card to the sender for the first and the
mark; show a work every tool within the authority it carries; give words no one is established for
a stranger's say, never none; and leave ADR-0390's rule along names to a later decision for
memories.

### 2. Two checks on a call, in order

> **Normative.** The check (ADR-0367 §4:1) asks two things of a call, in this order. First, may this
> work call the tool at its current authority: whether a grant covering the call applies for each
> person in its say, at the level of its context. Second, may the tool run: whether the grant that
> applies allows the call or asks.

> **Normative.** Where the first falls short only because the level of the work's context is below
> a covering grant's bar (ADR-0367 §5:3, ADR-0379 §2:5), or because another in its say may not make
> the call (ADR-0391 §3:3), the call needs the **first card**, to the person whose authority the
> work carries.

> **Normative.** A call short on both grounds takes one first card, at the bar ADR-0379 §2:5 gives
> for that person: its ticket's call is checked for the tapper alone, at the tap's level (ADR-0367
> §5:5).

> **Normative.** Where the second asks, a grant that asks applying and none that allows (ADR-0379
> §2:4), the call needs the **second card**, to the person the permission names: whoever the call's
> thing belongs to, by its record (ADR-0367 §5:6, ADR-0381 §6:1), or, for a call with no thing, the
> person ADR-0381 §6:2 names. Neither a grant's who nor who set it names whom to ask.

> **Normative.** A call that needs both cards takes the first before the second: ADR-0379 §2:5
> counts a grant that asks alike, so at a level at which no covering grant applies the outcome is a
> shortfall, and the first card's ticket, checked again, comes back needing the second as a new
> request (ADR-0367 §5:5 and §5's note).

> **Normative.** Both checks are the one check ADR-0367 §4:5 puts first among ADR-0306 §4:1's; the
> policy, the outside-content stand-in and the place follow as merged. The mark checks every send
> beside them (ADR-0370 §5:1, ADR-0391 §6:1).

> **Normative.** Where a send needs both the mark's yes (ADR-0370 §5:3) and the first card, and whom
> its work is for is the person whose authority it carries, the two are **one card** to that
> person: one request for the exact call (ADR-0361 §2), whose card shows the text the send carries
> (ADR-0370 §5:3), and which a tap answers only at the bar the first card needs (ADR-0367 §5:4).

> **Normative.** That card's yes passes the mark (ADR-0370 §5:5) and the first check, its ticket's
> call checked for the tapper alone at the tap's level (§2:3). A second card the call then needs
> comes after it and shows nothing beyond its exact call and `why` (ADR-0370 §5:5, §5:9).

The order is the merged one: §2:5 states what ADR-0379 §2 and ADR-0367 §5's note already give.
ADR-0370 §5:5 already lets the mark's yes cover a further card that shows nothing more; §2:7 makes
the mark's card and the first card one, where §5:8 had the mark's asked first and apart. Each card
covers its one call, in the owner's words "it should cover one call": a yes is a ticket for the
exact call, used once (ADR-0361 §5:1, §5:5), checked for its tapper (ADR-0367 §5:5), and no merged
clause raises a work's level or say on a tap (ADR-0368 §2:5, ADR-0391 §3:1). This decision adds no
rule to that. Where a send needs the mark's yes and the second card alone, ADR-0370 §5:8's order
stands.

### 3. A card to the person whose work it is

> **Normative.** A call that no grant covers for the person whose authority the work carries, or
> that a limit covers for them, is not done and asks no one (ADR-0367 §4:2), save the card ADR-0367
> §6:3 sends to the person whose own account it would act through: no card to the person whose
> authority the work carries stands in for a grant they lack.

> **Normative.** No card to the person whose authority a work carries makes a call allowed beyond
> what the lines allow that person at the tap's level (ADR-0367 §5:5, §10:3), and none stands in
> for a second card that names someone else (§2:4, ADR-0381 §6:1).

> **Normative.** A `text` call to a person the hub admits, into their own chat (ADR-0367 §12:1),
> takes no card for being allowed by a default grant alone: ADR-0367 §4:6's last sentence does not
> reach it, and every other check it passes stands.

The first card is no card to oneself in the owner's sense: it brings the work back up to its
person's own authority, which a fall in its level (ADR-0371 §6:1) or its say (ADR-0391 §3:1) took
it below, and never above it. ADR-0367 §5:3's card and ADR-0391 §3:3's stand, the latter as §2:3
adds to it. A second card that names the work's own person, for a call with no thing or a thing of
theirs, is the second check: their own grant asks, and the yes lifts nothing.

No policy rules a `text` call in the merged texts (ADR-0356 §7:7, as ADR-0367 §4:7 and ADR-0391 §6:1
amend it), and the built authorizer hands none to the policy, so ADR-0021 §5's floor never reached
one. §3:3 closes the reading of ADR-0367 §4:6 that had it ask (GAP-01). ADR-0021 §5's floor stands
whole, as the owner left it: a disclosing call the policy rules, such as `send_email`, under a
default line alone, still goes to its person's yes (ADR-0379 §3), and stops asking once their own
grant allows it (ADR-0021 §6, ADR-0379 §4).

### 4. Which tools a work is shown

> **Normative.** A work's **ceiling** is the authority it carries (ADR-0368 §2:2), at its sureness
> (ADR-0368 §2:3): the lines that apply to that person, or to each of its senders (ADR-0368 §7:3),
> with no one else in its say counted.

> **Normative.** ADR-0368 §8's *could use* reads the ceiling: a tool, a connection or a person is
> one the work's authority could use where some call on it could be allowed for the ceiling by the
> check, by the first card, by the second card, or under a ticket the work holds (ADR-0367 §5:5), or
> where the check would not reach it, as ADR-0368 §8 has it.

> **Normative.** A fall in the level of the work's context (ADR-0371 §6:1) or in its say (ADR-0391
> §3:1) hides nothing and shows nothing: the set ADR-0368 §8:1 fixes stands for the work, and a call
> the work no longer reaches takes the first card, where the work may ask (ADR-0368 §7:4).

> **Normative.** A tool a grant lets the ceiling's person only ask for is shown, and its call takes
> the second card.

> **Normative.** A tool on which no call could be allowed in any of §4:2's ways is not shown: one
> no grant covers for the ceiling, or a limit covers, or whose yes is no one's (ADR-0381 §5:2,
> §6:3) or cannot be asked in that work (ADR-0368 §7:4), where the work holds no ticket that could
> run a call of it and the check reaches it.

ADR-0368 §8:2 and §8:3 read *could use* as §4:2 has it, and ADR-0368 §8:4 to §8:6 stand. Its "at
its level" was the sureness ADR-0368 §2:3 records, which §4:1 keeps.

### 5. The owner's worked case

The owner's words, verbatim: "The model sees only the tools that are within the ceiling of
authority for that work. This is what the first card does and it answer the question, can this tool
even be called. Then there is can this tool even be run, which is where it may ask for permission.
So for the text Sam case, lets say Sam is the owner. I ask the assistant to text sam and to search
something up. Now it gets all of the tools under the authority ceiling, which is just my authority.
Then it does the web search, lowers the authority score, now when it tries to do the text sam tool
call, it send me a card to raise it back to high enough authority. Than it runs the tool call. Sam
has it setup that my authority isnt high enough to run the tool call, but it high enough to ask for
permission, so it sends a card to same to get the remaining authority. Sound good"

In this corpus's terms, with Sam the owner and the speaker a person the hub admits: the work carries
the speaker's authority, so the search and the text are both offered (§4:1, §4:2). The search lowers
the level of the work's context (ADR-0371 §6:1), and the text into Sam's chat then falls short of
its covering grant's bar: the first card, to the speaker (§2:2), which also asks the mark's yes
where the text carries more than Sam may see (§2:7). On that tap the ticket's call is checked for
the speaker at the tap's level, and Sam's line on his chat, a grant that asks applying for the
speaker, decides it: the second card, to Sam, whose chat it is, showing only what the speaker's card
showed (§2:4, §2:8). On Sam's yes it runs under its ticket (ADR-0361 §5, ADR-0367 §5:5).

### 6. No say is none

> **Normative.** A say is a set of people the hub admits, with or without a stranger, and never
> **none**. A work's say holds a stranger where any block in its context carries one or counts as
> one in that work (ADR-0391 §2:1, §3:1).

> **Normative.** Forwarded, quoted or relayed words whose writer a medium does not establish as a
> person the hub admits carry a stranger's say (ADR-0391 §2:6, §5:2). ADR-0391 §5:2's kind that
> declares a most of none, a level, still establishes no one.

> **Normative.** A model's relay mark lowers what an input counts at in that work alone to a
> stranger's say (ADR-0391 §5:1, §8:2); the say recorded on the text is unchanged.

> **Normative.** An input holding such words, or marked relayed, carries the authority ADR-0368
> §3:2 gives its author where its author is established as that person and sent it as an
> instruction, its parties being that person, the writers of such words, or both, so a forward
> sent on its own counts (ADR-0351 §3): the words add a stranger to the work's say and take no
> authority away. They carry none of their own (ADR-0351 §6:2).

> **Normative.** No work carries no authority for its say. ADR-0368 §7:5 holds for a work whose
> facts give none, an approval's outcome's and upkeep's among them, and ADR-0391 §6:4 for those.

> **Normative.** This section changes no level: ADR-0366 §5:1's none and every level a text or
> input counts at stay as ADR-0366, ADR-0368 §2:6 and ADR-0371 give them.

So a forward's work, or one holding pasted words its model marked relayed, does what both its
author and a stranger may at the level of its context; a call its author may make and a stranger
may not takes the first card (ADR-0391 §3:3, §2:2) rather than being refused with no one asked; a
call its author may not make is not done. It reads no more than anyone may see (ADR-0391 §3:4),
as before; it replies under the reply line, checked for its author and a stranger (ADR-0391 §6:1);
and it is shown its author's tools (§4:1). ADR-0391 §3:4's and §8:3's limbs for a say of none
reach nothing.

### 7. The rule along names waits for memories

> **Normative.** Until a later decision on how a memory is written, ADR-0390 §5:5 does not hold
> where the record naming or the record named is a memory: a name between a memory and another
> record bounds no score and carries no narrowing or widening along it.

> **Normative.** A memory's score is still set when it is written as ADR-0390 §3 rules, changed by
> a write as §3:2 rules, and changed by a person's act as §4 rules.

> **Normative.** Until that decision, a reader sees by its home alone (ADR-0383 §6:2), not by its
> score (ADR-0390 §5:4), a memory, every stored record that names a memory, and every stored record
> that names a record seen so, so no record a reader is shown names a record the reader does not
> see (ADR-0383 §8:3).

Between every two stored records neither of which is a memory, episodes among them, ADR-0390 §5:5
holds as written. §7:3 is this decision's: ADR-0390 §9 rests ADR-0383 §8:3's conclusion on §5:5,
and without §5:5 a memory seen by its score could name an episode its reader does not see, and a
pin seen by its score a memory. A name joins records of one home (ADR-0383 §4:7), so a reader who
sees one record by its home sees every record it names, and a record seen by its score names none
seen by its home alone. A record §7:3 reaches keeps its score, which still bounds whom it may be
sent to (ADR-0390 §2:2).

## Consequences

- A call that falls short of its work's authority asks the person whose work it is, once, for the
  mark too; one whose permission asks asks the person it names.
- A work is shown what its person could do or ask for, so a falling score hides nothing.
- A forward or relayed words bound a work to what a stranger may do and see, and a card gets back
  what its author may do; they never cost it everything.
- Until memories are written by their own design, a memory, and every record that names one or
  names a record so seen, is seen by its home alone.
- **Revisit** once a second person's cards are built, or once the memory decision lands (§7).
- **Set aside:** a card to the sender wherever only a default grant allows a message (it asks no one
  who can supply anything); hiding a tool whose permission only asks (the model could not ask); and
  a say of none apart from a stranger's (it refuses where a card would do).
